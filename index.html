04Emergency Response — On-Call Staffing for a Major Outage

If a major outage hits (a burst of mostly-High-priority tickets, 45/hr for 2 hours), normal day staffing (6 agents) isn't enough — SLA attainment during the outage window drops to 40.8%. This sweep asks: how many agents on-call would actually hold the line?

11on-call agents
is the point SLA attainment during the outage crosses 90% (91.5%) — the recommended minimum surge/on-call response for a major incident of this size, roughly +5 agents above normal staffing.
Model: SimPy discrete-event simulation · Poisson ticket arrivals · exponential service times · 30 Monte Carlo replications/scenario
Emergency scenario: team-assumed major-outage shape (2-hour burst, 45 tickets/hr, 70% High priority) — a modeling choice, not derived from data
Priority mix (20% High / 50% Medium / 30% Low), service times, and SLA targets are labeled team assumptions.
